Trading Configuration

Differs · 1 tick

When any watched market prints the same last digit twice in a row, the bot instantly buys one Differs on that digit, then keeps watching. One trade at a time. After a loss, the next trade is always on a different market. Differs wins about 90% of the time but pays only a small amount, so one loss costs far more than a win earns. Martingale can rapidly increase risk. Test on a demo account first.
